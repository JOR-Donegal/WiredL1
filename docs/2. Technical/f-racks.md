# 2. Racks
All our equipment is fitted to 19” racks, these are a standard. All equipment height is standardized in height units or “U”, where 1U is 44.5mm.

For a very small site, where we have <48 CAT6 points, we might use a small wall mounted cabinet, colloquially referred to as a “microwave oven cabinet”. This might be configured with 48 x CAT6 outlets and a 48-port switch. We would also keep the DSL equipment here for internet connectivity and it would also have the main phone line.

For a larger site, we will use a full-size rack. This will be specified in U and a 42U cabinet is typical. This kind of rack is perfect for mounting all kinds of network and data equipment. These racks are typically 600mm wide, but may be 800mm wide to allow cable tidies to be installed on either side. Data centre standards mandate the 800w cabinets. Racks are normally 600mm deep for use with structured cabling but will be >=1000mm deep if they are to be used with servers. Some server equipment requires even deeper cabinets, check in every case what your requirements will be. If we have a row of cabinets, we might leave out the doors in between them an “bay” them together with special bolts called baying kits.

Keep in mind that this is a very simple summary. Before you purchase racks, you need to determine the environment in which they will be sited and their IP rating. You need to decide how much power will terminate in each rack. What will the weight of the rack be, how many racks and other, can the floor support that weight? 

In a data center, this all gets much more complicated. Enterprise data centers may be of any size and in recent times many companies have even made them design features of a building. At commercial scale, rows of racks will be mounted back-to-back in a cold aisle / hot aisle arrangement in computer rooms up to 25m x 25m in size. There may be many such rooms in a data center. 

At the cloud scale, all of this goes out the window! We have lorry container-sized modules and most of the standards we have discussed do not apply.
